# Portfolio Storytelling Guide

## One-sentence description

A time-validated construction machine-learning case study that predicts cost overruns and schedule delays from early project-controls and workflow indicators.

## Interview narrative

1. Projects 1 and 2 established cost-control and workflow analytics foundations.
2. Project 3 converted those feature concepts into a leakage-controlled predictive dataset.
3. The project used train, validation, and future test periods.
4. Transparent and nonlinear models were compared.
5. The cost model generalized strongly.
6. Schedule performance degraded in the future test period and was explicitly documented.
7. Dashboards translate probability into project review priority.
8. The Act phase establishes monitoring, human review, and retraining controls.

## Responsible positioning

The project demonstrates machine-learning methodology and governance. It does not claim production deployment on actual construction clients.
