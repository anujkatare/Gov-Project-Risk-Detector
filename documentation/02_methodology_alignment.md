# Methodology Alignment

| Project phase | CRISP-DM | DMAIC | Microsoft data-science lifecycle | PMI governance terminology |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ask | Business Understanding | Define | Business Understanding | Initiating and early Planning |
| Prepare | Data Understanding | Measure | Data Acquisition and Understanding | Planning |
| Process | Data Preparation | Measure and early Analyze | Data Acquisition and Understanding | Executing; Monitoring and Controlling |
| Analyze | Modeling and Evaluation | Analyze | Modeling | Executing; Monitoring and Controlling |
| Share | Evaluation | Analyze and improvement design | Customer Acceptance | Stakeholder engagement; Monitoring and Controlling |
| Act | Deployment | Improve and Control | Deployment and Customer Acceptance | Executing; Monitoring and Controlling; transition; Closing |

The frameworks are conceptually aligned but are not treated as identical or strictly sequential.

## Public methodology wording

> Methodology: Google Data Analytics six-phase framework—Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, and Act—supported by terminology and practices from CRISP-DM, DMAIC, Microsoft’s data-science lifecycle, and PMI project-governance process groups.
